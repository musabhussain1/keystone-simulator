
==============================================================================
KEYSTONE PLACEMENT: ANALYSIS RUN
==============================================================================
Each customer is in the choose half or the test half, never both.
A shelf is scored on the test half only: how many of every 100 orders it fully stocks.
Each simulated week changes at most 10% of the shelf spaces. Weeks simulated: 12.
The cost test of Step 2 is not applied: these datasets have no prices or costs.
